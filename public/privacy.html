Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Math Scratchpad is a personal educational application for recording mathematics practice, lesson progress, answers, and study activity. This policy explains what information the application handles and how it is used.

Information processed

  • Learning data: practice events, question and lesson identifiers, submitted answers and steps, correctness when supplied by a checker, confidence, hints, and session timing or progress information.
  • Google account information: when you choose Google sign-in, the application receives an identity token to verify that the signed-in account is the application owner.
  • Google Drive data: when you connect Drive and select a file or backup folder, the application accesses only the files or folders authorized through Google's drive.file permission. The question bank remains in your selected Drive file. Sync backups are stored in the private Drive location selected or created for this purpose.
  • Notion data: if the owner enables the optional Notion mirror, learning events may be sent to the configured private Notion databases to maintain study records and reports.

Where information is stored

Learning data is first stored in the browser's local storage/database on the device. If synchronization is enabled, the application sends learning events to its Cloudflare Worker and owner-scoped Durable Object storage. Optional mirrors copy configured learning records to the owner's private Google Drive and Notion workspace. Google and Notion process information under their own privacy terms.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used only to provide the application's learning, synchronization, backup, restore, and reporting features. Math Scratchpad does not sell personal information or use learning records for advertising. Data is not intentionally shared with other users. The application does not request broad access to all Google Drive files.

Retention and deletion

Browser data remains on the device until cleared by the user or the browser. Server-side synchronization records and mirrored copies may remain until the owner deletes them from the relevant service. Disconnecting an account or disabling synchronization does not automatically delete previously stored copies. To request help with data held by the application, contact the address below.

Security

Requests to the sync service are restricted to the configured owner account and use authenticated Google identity tokens. Secrets are kept in server-side secret storage and are not included in the application bundle. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This application is a personal learning tool and is not designed to collect information from children through a public account-registration service.

Changes and contact
